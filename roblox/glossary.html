§ 15  ·  Reference

Glossary

Every term, metric, segment and threshold in this manual, defined once and sorted alphabetically.

TermAreaDefinition
Active payer statusSegmentPercentile-based spender segmentation within your game, recalculated daily: Top 15% (85th–100th percentile of your spenders), Intermediate 35% (50th–84th), Casual 50% (0th–49th), Lapsed (no spend in 30 days), Never.
Active SpenderSegmentA user who has made qualifying purchases totalling at least $9.99 anywhere on Roblox within the past 60 days, and who was not a new or reactivated user during that period. The unit of Creator Rewards eligibility.
AnalyticsServiceAPIThe engine service used to log funnel, economy and custom events. Server-side only, and only in published games — never from the client or from Studio.
ARPDAUMonetizationAverage revenue per daily active user. Equals payer conversion rate × ARPPU. Benchmarked against similar games.
ARPPUMonetizationAverage revenue per paying user — your spend depth. Platform median is roughly $0.70 per game; the 99th percentile is around $55.
ARPUMonetizationAverage revenue per user, reported in experiment results. Revenue divided by all players, not just payers.
Audience Expansion RewardsCreator RewardsA 35% revenue share on a newly acquired or reactivated user's first $100 of qualifying purchases anywhere on Roblox during their first 60 days, when attributed to you.
Benchmark setAnalyticsThe comparison pool for your KPIs: games with similar players, or your genre, or all experiences — depending on available data. Can change over time; transitions are annotated on your charts.
Beta modePublishingAn audience setting that excludes your game from Recommended for You. All other analytics keep working, but Home recommendation metrics read zero.
CCUEngagementConcurrent users — players in your game at one moment. Publicly visible. A scale readout, not a health readout.
CohortRetentionA group of users sharing a first-play date. The x-axis on all retention charts is the cohort's first play date, not the return date.
Compute efficiencyPerformanceYour server efficiency versus the Roblox average. Roblox states explicitly that it is not indicative of a good or bad player experience.
ConfigsToolingValues you can adjust in a live game without publishing a new version — progression rates, rewards, prices.
Conversion rateMonetizationPercent of daily active users who are also paying users. Platform-wide, roughly 3.8% of tracked players pay anything at all.
CPPAdsCost per play. Total ad campaign spend divided by plays. The primary efficiency metric in Ads Manager.
Creator RewardsCreator RewardsThe programme that replaced Engagement-Based Payouts in July 2025, comprising Daily Engagement Rewards and Audience Expansion Rewards.
Custom eventInstrumentationA game-specific event logged with LogCustomEvent, as a counter or with a numeric value. Limit: 100 unique event names.
Custom fieldInstrumentationUp to three string dimensions attached to funnel, economy and custom events for breakdown and filtering. Up to 8,000 unique value combinations before grouping as “Other”.
D1 / D7 / D30 retentionRetentionPercentage of new users who return the next day, on the 8th day, and on the 31st day. Map to core loop / progression system / endgame respectively.
Daily Engagement RewardsCreator Rewards5 Robux per day per Active Spender who plays your game for 10+ minutes, provided it is one of the first three experiences they launch that day.
DAU / WAU / MAUEngagementUnique players in a day, week or month. DAU ÷ MAU is your stickiness.
Developer productMonetizationA repeatable, consumable purchase. The main engine of spend days per user.
Economy eventInstrumentationA logged source or sink of an in-game resource, with amount, post-transaction balance, transaction type and optional item SKU. Unlocks the Economy dashboard.
ExperimentsToolingA/B tests on config values or matchmaking, running 14–60 days, reporting playtime, session time, ARPU, ARPPU and retention with confidence intervals.
Explore pageAnalyticsCustom visualisation builder: choose a source and metric, break down by segment or custom field, define calculated metrics with formulas, overlay benchmarks or prior periods, apply a 7-day moving average.
First play bounce rateRanking signalThe rate at which users leave after a short first session. A negative ranking signal, measured in two buckets: under 60 seconds and 61–180 seconds. Second in Roblox's published priority order.
Funnel eventInstrumentationA logged step in a sequence. One-time funnels use LogOnboardingFunnelStepEvent; recurring funnels use LogFunnelStepEvent with a funnelSessionId. Limit: 10 funnels, 100 steps each.
funnelSessionIdInstrumentationAn identifier distinguishing separate runs through a recurring funnel. Only the 10 most recent unique values per user per funnel are tracked.
GetPlayerSegmentsAsyncAPIQueries a player's analytics segments at runtime so you can adapt offers, onboarding and gameplay per player.
In-experience activity statusSegmentLifecycle within your game: Early (first 30 days), Active, Lapsed (no sessions in 30 days), Reactivated, Never played.
Intentional co-play days per userRanking signalAverage unique days users return to play with friends — via join, invite or private server. Second-tier ranking signal.
Item SKUInstrumentationYour own identifier for an item in an economy event. Optional; shows as N/A if omitted. Grouped as “Other” after 100 unique values.
Lapsed userSegmentIn Creator Rewards terms, a user who has not logged on to Roblox for 60 consecutive days.
Like ratioFeedbackShare of upvotes versus downvotes. Widely treated as a ranking factor by the community; Roblox has never listed it as one. Treat it as a conversion input.
MDEExperimentsMinimum detectable effect — the smallest change an experiment can reliably detect, given goal metric, DAU, rollout percentage, duration and variant splits.
New user first session retentionEngagementThe percentage of new users still playing X minutes after joining for the first time. The highest-resolution onboarding view Roblox provides.
Out-of-memory exitPerformanceA session ended by an OOM error or low-memory warning. A spike after an update indicates a memory regression.
PassMonetizationA one-time purchase granting a permanent effect. Contrast with developer products, which are consumable.
Platform spender statusSegmentFixed-threshold segmentation across all of Roblox: Active ($9.99+ spent anywhere in 60 days) or Other. Matches the Creator Rewards definition.
Play days per userRanking signalAverage unique days users engage with your game, measured across D1, D2–7 and D8–28. Third in the published priority order.
Play through rateRanking signalThe rate at which users play your game after seeing it in Recommended for You. Roblox's highest-priority ranking signal, and the only one with no time segments.
Playtime per userRanking signalAverage time users spend in your game, capped at 60 minutes per user, per game, per day for ranking purposes. Fourth in the published priority order.
Qualified playRanking signalA meaningful play session, with accidental clicks and quick bounces filtered out. Roblox does not publish the qualifying threshold.
Qualified play sessions per userRanking signalAverage number of qualified sessions per user who clicked through from Home recommendations. Second-tier ranking signal.
RankingDiscoveryStage two of Recommended for You: ordering the candidate set for a specific user, using the eight signals measured only on organically acquired players.
Recommended for YouDiscoveryThe personalised Home sort that supplies most discovery traffic on Roblox.
RetrievalDiscoveryStage one of Recommended for You: narrowing all games to a candidate set. Any traffic source can accelerate consideration here.
Robux spent per userRanking signalAverage Robux spent per user. The lowest-priority of the eight published signals.
Server heartbeatPerformanceServer frame rate, capped at 60 FPS. Drops reduce client frame rate and increase latency.
Share linkAcquisitionA unique trackable URL to your game, letting you attribute off-platform traffic and compare channels on retention and revenue rather than volume.
Spend days per userRanking signalAverage unique days on which users spend Robux. Listed before Robux spent per user — frequency is weighted above size.
StickinessEngagementDAU divided by MAU. The share of your monthly audience present on an average day.
Thumbnail personalisationAcquisitionShowing each user the most relevant of your 2–5 active thumbnails. Average qualified play through rate lift of +8.5%, up to +50% for some games.
When user first playedSegmentCohort-age segmentation: 0–30, 31–90, 91–180, 181–365, 365+ days ago, or never. The single most useful filter for diagnosing an update.
A note on the ranking signals

Nine entries above are marked Ranking signal. Those eight signals plus play through rate are the complete published list of what feeds the Recommended for You ranking stage. Everything else in this glossary is either an input to one of them, a diagnostic for one of them, or context.
