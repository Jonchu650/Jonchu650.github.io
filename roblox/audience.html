§ 08  ·  Metrics

Audience & feedback

Who is actually playing, how to slice every other chart by them, and how to read the two qualitative signals Roblox gives you.

01Demographics

Analytics › Demographics breaks your monthly active users down five ways.

DimensionDetail
CountryPercent of MAU by country. Hover for absolute MAU; download for the full country list
GenderPercent of MAU by gender
AgePercent of MAU by age band
Top languagesThe top 10 languages spoken by your MAU, with percentages
Server frame rateIncluded on this page — frames per second processed by the server
For privacy, any age or gender group with fewer than three users is hidden from the charts.

What to do with it

Prioritise content

Build for the audience you have, not the one you imagined. If your player base skews younger than your design assumptions, your difficulty curve and text density are probably wrong.

Localise

Translate your metadata — title, description — with the top languages in mind. The dashboard's language filter then lets you measure whether a translation actually moved anything.

Find the gap

A country with high MAU and below-average retention is usually a localisation or performance problem, not a taste problem. Check device mix for that region before concluding anything.

Sell the audience

Demographics data is what brand sponsors ask for. It is the only page here with a use outside your own decision-making.

02Segmentation: the real power tool

Demographics is a page. Segmentation is a capability that applies to every chart on every analytics page. Filters persist across pages until you turn them off, which makes it easy to forget one is on — worth checking when numbers look inexplicable.

The two segments worth building a habit around

When user first played and Source. The first separates a ‘retention drop’ caused by breaking onboarding from one caused by a flood of casual new players. The second separates behaviour the algorithm reads from behaviour it ignores. Check both after every update, before you form a theory.

Cohort maturity

In a newly launched game, most DAU comes from players who joined in the last 30 days. As a game matures, older cohorts should make up a growing share. Break Engagement down by When user first played and watch the shape over months: stable or growing older cohorts mean healthy long-term engagement; if your 0–30 day cohort is stable but the 181–365 day cohort is falling, acquisition is fine and your long-term players are leaving. That is an endgame and content-cadence problem.

The chart view shows total feedback and percent upvotes across a selected range (7/30/60/90 days or custom), with a relative comparison against the preceding equivalent period. Hovering a relative percentage reveals the exact dates used.

Two caveats that matter

Players can change their rating at any time, and ratings update at the end of each day — so the dashboard may not match the live rating on your game page. Do not treat same-day feedback movement as real.

Also: a downvote with a detailed comment is worth more than an upvote with none. Roblox flags this directly. Read the comments on the negatives.

Device and platform on every row

Easily the most under-used field in the feedback table. If your negative comments cluster on one platform, you do not have a design problem — you have a performance or UI-scaling problem, and the Performance dashboard will confirm it in about a minute.

04AI reports

Two automated report types, both gated behind scale.

ReportRequirementContents
Analytics report (weekly / monthly)1,000+ DAU. Monthly in the first week of each month, weekly otherwiseTop drivers of revenue and DAU change; automatic outlier detection across age, country and platform; player feedback summaries
Player feedback report20+ feedback commentsWhat players like, suggestions for improvement, feature requests
Analytics reports are built with Gemini 2.5; feedback reports with Meta Llama 3. Roblox warns that both can make mistakes and should be weighed against your own judgment.

The genuinely useful part is automatic outlier detection — it surfaces the kind of finding you would only reach by manually filtering twenty combinations, such as “retention dropped significantly more on iOS, and among 18+ users.” If you are under 1,000 DAU you do not get this, so do it by hand: after any significant move, check the metric segmented by platform, age band and country before you theorise.

05Insights and achievements

The game overview page shows insights for games with 100+ DAU — daily-updated callouts highlighting large moves in key metrics, with comparison against similar games. Roblox's own example: your revenue declined 21% while similar games rose 15%. That contrast is the useful part; an isolated −21% could be seasonality.

The achievement panel flags six-month highs. Worth noting Roblox's own caveat: a metric can be at a six-month high and still be below benchmark. A personal best is not the same as good.

06The audience checklist

  • You know your top three countries and top three languages by MAU
  • You know your dominant device class, and you have played your own game on it
  • You check When user first played and Source after every update
  • You read the feedback comments — especially the negative ones with detail
  • You have checked whether negative feedback clusters on one platform
  • No stale filter is left applied across your dashboard
