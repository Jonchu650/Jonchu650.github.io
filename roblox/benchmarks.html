§ 11  ·  Practice

Benchmarks

What the numbers actually are, where each one comes from, and why three credible sources can disagree by a factor of three about the same metric.

Read this before using any number on this page

Roblox's own similar-games benchmark, shown on your dashboard, beats everything here. It is computed daily against games enjoyed by players similar to yours, which controls for genre, audience age and region simultaneously. Nothing on a public web page can do that.

Use these figures for sanity-checking and for the period before you have 100 DAU and any benchmarks at all.

01How Roblox's own benchmarks work

Two different benchmarking systems, for two different kinds of metric.

SystemApplies toHow it works
Similar-player benchmarksAll retention KPIs; average session time; ARPPU, ARPDAU and conversion rate; play through rateA percentile band from games with similar players, shown as 50th–90th percentile. Updated daily. Requires 100+ DAU
Top-N tiersOverall success KPIsBenchmarked against the top 1000 games by total playtime over a rolling 30 days, excluding games under 30 days old. Shown as Top 200 / Top 500 / Top 1000 tiers

The benchmark set can change under you

Depending on available data you may be compared against games with similar players, or against your genre, or against all experiences if your game has no genre label yet. Games move between sets as Roblox gathers data, and the dashboard annotates the transition directly on the chart.

This matters because a set change can move your apparent percentile without anything about your game changing. Check for the annotation before concluding you improved or regressed.

How to read a band

A D1 retention benchmark of 12.11%–18.73% means half of comparable games are at or below 12.11%, and the top 10% are at or above 18.73%. Landing at 15% puts you comfortably above median. The band is a distribution, not a target.

The benchmark trap that wastes the most time

Reading a “Simulator: 32% D1” figure, comparing it to your unlaunched game, and concluding you have a catastrophe. Across the whole platform the 99th percentile for D1 is around 22%. If your game is at 14%, you are in roughly the 85th percentile of all Roblox games, and your actual problem is probably somewhere else entirely.

07Setting your own targets

  • Before 100 DAU: you have no benchmarks. Use the platform-wide medians as a floor — roughly 10% D1, roughly 10-minute sessions — and focus on instrumentation instead.
  • At 100–1,000 DAU: your similar-games benchmark appears. Target the 50th percentile on your weakest metric first, not the 90th on your strongest.
  • Above 1,000 DAU: experiments become viable and AI reports unlock. Move to testing rather than guessing.
  • Always: compare against your own previous cohorts as well as the benchmark. Your trend is more actionable than your percentile.
The most useful single target

Get your median session length above 10 minutes. It is the only number on Roblox with a direct, published financial consequence attached (Creator Rewards), it sits right at the platform median so it is realistically reachable, and the work that gets you there — cutting early friction — simultaneously improves bounce rate, qualified sessions and D1 retention.
