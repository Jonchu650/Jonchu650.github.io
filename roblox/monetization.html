Monetization

Two of the eight ranking signals are monetary, and they reward frequency over size. That single fact should reshape how you price things.

01The decomposition

Every monetization conversation should start here, because revenue never moves for a mysterious reason.

Revenue   =  DAU  ×  ARPDAU
ARPDAU    =  payer conversion rate  ×  ARPPU
MetricDefinitionBenchmarked?
RevenueHourly and daily revenue, split by developer products, passes and commissionsNo
Conversion ratePercent of daily active users who are also paying usersYes
Paying usersNumber of DAU who purchased a game-related itemNo
ARPPUAverage revenue per paying user — your spend depthYes
ARPDAUAverage revenue per daily active user — overall monetization healthYes
Found under Monetization › Overview. Conversion, ARPPU and ARPDAU all carry similar-game benchmark bands at 100+ DAU.
The diagnostic that saves the most time

High ARPPU with low ARPDAU means your revenue comes from a narrow slice of players. Roblox names this pattern explicitly. The fix is not more expensive items for your whales — it is widening the base. Conversely, decent ARPDAU with low ARPPU means plenty of people buy but nobody goes deep, and you need higher-value options.

02What the numbers are

3.80%Share of tracked players who paid anything at all, platform-wide
~50%Share of revenue from the top 15% of active payers, on average
$0.81Median spend by a paying player, platform-wide

Two facts to hold together: fewer than one player in twenty-five ever spends anything, and about half your revenue will come from roughly one-seventh of the people who do. Your monetization design has to serve both realities at once.

03Payer segmentation

Roblox exposes two different spender segmentations and they are easy to confuse.

SegmentBasisValues
Active payer statusPercentile-based, within your game, recalculated dailyTop 15% (85th–100th percentile of your spenders) · Intermediate 35% (50th–84th) · Casual 50% (0th–49th) · Lapsed (no spend in 30 days) · Never
Platform spender statusFixed threshold, across all of RobloxActive — spent $9.99 or more anywhere on Roblox in the last 60 days · Other
Platform spender status matters because it matches the Creator Rewards definition of an Active Spender — the players who earn you Daily Engagement Rewards.

How to use it: go to Monetization › Overview, break down by Active Payer Status, and check it across several time intervals. If your top 15% contributes materially less than half your revenue, you have room to retain or monetize top spenders better. If it contributes materially more, your mid- and low-tier value proposition is too thin and you are one whale's departure from a bad quarter.

04Raising payer conversion

Payer conversion rate

Roblox says fix this secondBenchmarked

The percent of your users who engage with paid features or content at all.

Why it leads
A player who makes one purchase is much more likely to purchase again
Feeds
Spend days per user (ranking signal 07) and Robux spent per user (08)
Diagnose with
A shop funnel — open store → view item → add to cart → purchase
  • Find out why it is low first. Roblox lists three usual suspects: items priced too high, poor item or shop visibility, and content nobody wants.
  • Welcome offers and first-purchase discounts. Lowering the barrier to the first purchase is the highest-leverage monetization change available, because the first purchase is what predicts the rest.
  • Analyse the purchase funnel. Unclear steps and distractions mid-flow kill completions. Instrument it as a recurring funnel with a funnelSessionId.
  • Treat the storefront as a designed space. Roblox's phrasing: devote as much creativity and attention to your shop as to the rest of the game.

05Raising ARPPU

  • Mix one-time and repeatable purchases. Durables for commitment, consumables and currency for repeat spend. A shop of only durables has a hard revenue ceiling per player.
  • Widen the catalogue. If there are only a few items, a willing spender runs out of things to buy. Find your top sellers and expand around them.
  • Seasonal items. Holidays and real-world events create urgency and a reason to look at the shop again.

06Raising ARPDAU

  • Multiple price points. Some players will only ever spend a small amount. A wide ladder captures them instead of excluding them.
  • Improve retention and engagement. More sessions means more purchase opportunities and more investment in the game. Retention is a monetization lever, not a separate concern.
Price ladders are an algorithm strategy

The ranking signals include spend days per user — the number of unique days a player spends anything — listed before Robux spent per user. One 1,000 Robux gamepass produces one spend day. Ten 80 Robux consumables spread across ten days produce ten.

Same revenue, ten times the signal. If you take one monetization idea from this manual, take this one: build a cheap, repeatable, frequently-refreshed purchase into your economy, even if it is not where the money is.

08Creator Rewards

Creator Rewards replaced Engagement-Based Payouts in July 2025. Two mechanisms, and the first one sets a hard design target.

Daily Engagement Rewards

Sets a 10-minute target

5 Robux per day, per Active Spender, if your game is one of the first three experiences they launch that day and they play it for 10+ minutes.

Active Spender
Has spent $9.99+ anywhere on Roblox in the past 60 days, and is not a new or reactivated user
Threshold
10 minutes in a day, cumulative across sessions
Constraint
Must be one of the first three experiences launched that day
Implication
Median session length for this segment must clear 10 minutes or most of it earns nothing

Roblox's own worked examples make the rule concrete: a player who spends 2 minutes in one game, 5 in another, 10 in a third, 5 in a fourth and 10 more in the first earns rewards for the games where they crossed 10 minutes — but a fifth game they played for 15 minutes earns nothing, because it was the fourth experience they launched that day.

Check this in your own dashboard

Filter Acquisition by Platform spender status → Active spender and review Unique users with plays by source. Then check Engagement › Average Playtime for the same segment. If average playtime for active spenders sits near or below 10 minutes, a large share of your qualifying players never actually qualify — and closing that gap is pure upside with no pricing risk attached.

Audience Expansion Rewards

Bring a genuinely new user to Roblox, or reactivate one lapsed 60+ days, and you earn a 35% revenue share on their first $100 of qualifying purchases anywhere on the platform during their first 60 days.

Why share links matter here

Attribution is what makes this pay. Off-platform traffic without a share link is attributed to nobody in particular.

09Economy balance

If you instrument economy events you unlock the Economy dashboard, which is where in-game currency problems become visible before they become retention problems.

ChartHealthy looks likeAction if not
Total sources and sinks by categorySources minus sinks close to zeroIf the net total is growing, add sinks
Average wallet balanceFlat or slowly rising, similar shape for payers and non-payersGrowing balances — especially for payers — means you need new sinks
Top sources and sinksIn-app purchases visible among sourcesIf nobody sources currency from IAP, prices are too high or the options are wrong
All sources and sinksA combined view over your chosen rangeUse for month-over-month drift

Currency inflation is a slow failure mode: balances rise, prices feel irrelevant, purchases stop mattering, and conversion quietly decays over months. The Economy dashboard is the only early-warning system you get, and it requires instrumentation you have to add yourself.

10Regional pricing and sales data

Roblox supports regional pricing so the same item can cost differently by market. Worth enabling before you conclude a region simply does not spend.

For deeper analysis, download the sales CSV: Transactions › Sales of Goods › Download Data, one calendar month at a time, up to two years back. It requires a verified email and an authenticator app as your 2-step method, and arrives as a download link that expires in 48 hours. Each row carries buyer user ID, timestamp, sale location, universe, asset, asset type, hold status, revenue and price — enough to do cohort and pricing analysis that the dashboard will not do for you.

One useful thing to do with the CSV

Filter for pending Robux to work out when your payout actually releases. Useful for planning a DevEx cash-out or paying collaborators, and not available anywhere in the dashboard UI.
