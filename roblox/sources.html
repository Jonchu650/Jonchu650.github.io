§ 16  ·  Reference

Sources

Where every claim in this manual came from, what was taken from each source, and how far each one should be trusted.

The rule this manual follows

Mechanics, definitions, thresholds and access requirements come from Roblox's official creator documentation and are stated as fact. Benchmark numbers come from third parties and are always labelled with their sample. Where the community believes something Roblox has not confirmed — like-ratio weighting, qualified-play thresholds, revenue-per-CCU rules of thumb — it is flagged as unconfirmed in the sentence where it appears.

03What this manual does not claim to know

QuestionStatus
The relative weight of each ranking signalRoblox publishes two tiers and an order within them, but not numeric weights. This manual shows tiers, not invented weights
The qualified-play thresholdNot published. Community estimates circulate; none is confirmed
Whether like ratio feeds the algorithmNever listed as a ranking signal by Roblox. It plainly affects play through rate via the details page, which is a different mechanism
Revenue per CCU rules of thumbWidely quoted, never sourced. Use the DAU and revenue identities on the rundown page and your own numbers instead
Exact retrieval-stage mechanicsRoblox describes the inputs qualitatively. The specifics are not public
How long a content-quality penalty lastsRoblox says content is reclassified with every update and the banner refreshes daily, but gives no timeline

04Rebuilding this manual

The manual is generated from a small Python build script with one module per page — no dependencies, no build tooling. To edit a page, change the corresponding build/p_*.py file and run python3 build/build.py. To change the design, edit site/assets/style.css; the build does not overwrite it.

The chart colours were validated for colour-vision deficiency and contrast in both light and dark modes before use, and are documented at the top of the stylesheet.
