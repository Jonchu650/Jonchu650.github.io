§ 05  ·  Metrics

Acquisition

Where new and returning users come from, and how well each source converts. The dashboard that tells you whether your growth is real or rented.

Sequence matters more here than anywhere else

Roblox's instruction is unambiguous: before acquiring users — running ads, announcing an event, doing a creator push — make sure retention and engagement are already good. Sending traffic into a leaky game does not just waste the traffic; because the ranking signals are per-user averages, it actively worsens your organic position.

01The sources

Every play is attributed to one of these. Knowing the list matters because the behaviour of each cohort is genuinely different — a player from Search has intent, a player from Sponsored may not.

Beta mode zeroes your Home recommendations

If your game has beta mode enabled, all other analytics keep working but Home recommendation metrics read zero — games in beta are excluded from Recommended for You entirely. If your recommendation numbers are flat zero and you cannot work out why, check this first.

Judge a source by the last four columns, not the first. A channel that sends 10,000 players with 2% D7 is worth less than one sending 1,000 with 15%.

Raising it honestly

Roblox's own guidance and its suppression rules point the same way: PTR rises when your metadata is accurate and distinctive, and falls when it is generic, misleading, or leads with a reward.

  • A title that states the loop. The best-performing titles on Roblox function as the pitch and the tutorial at once — a player knows what they will do before they click.
  • An icon that reads at tile size. Test it at the actual rendered size, on a phone, in a grid of competitors.
  • Two to five active thumbnails, permanently. Thumbnail personalisation needs at least two to activate, and Roblox recommends keeping several live so it can adapt as audiences shift.
  • Images and video on the details page. Roblox lists this directly as a play-through lever.
  • Metadata that matches the game. Mismatch is an explicit suppression trigger, not just a conversion problem.
  • No monetary hooks in the title. Leading with implied rewards is explicitly deprioritised.

The thumbnail testing protocol

Roblox's recommended rhythm: test multiple new thumbnails with every major update, then leave them alone until the next one. Do not pick a single winner and delete the rest — Roblox shows a case where an apparently weak thumbnail later became the second-best performer. Personalisation keeps giving losers minimal traffic specifically so it can catch that.

04Improving acquisition from Roblox sources

This is the part creators most often try to solve with marketing, and Roblox's answer is that you mostly cannot. Its two listed levers for Home traffic are both game-quality levers:

  1. Improve engagement and retention — get session time and D1 retention to or above your similar-games benchmark. The algorithm cares because these signal how satisfying the game is.
  2. Optimise monetization — get payer conversion and ARPPU to or above benchmark. The algorithm cares because these measure how invested players are.

Beyond that, the levers are metadata (above) and social: experience events and player invite prompts bring friends in, which feeds both the Friends source and the co-play ranking signal.

05External traffic and share links

Off-platform traffic lands in Other and is invisible unless you create share links. A share link is a unique trackable URL to your game; the Share Links tab on Analytics Home reports Link Visits, and the acquisition funnel can be viewed by link instead of by source.

That turns every external channel into a measurable one. You can finally answer:

  • Users with qualified plays — how many real players each link brought
  • 7-day playtime per user — which channel brings players who actually engage
  • D7 retention — which platform's audience sticks
  • 30-day revenue per user — which channel brings players who spend
Make one share link per channel, per campaign

One for TikTok, one for each creator you work with, one per video if you can. The cost is zero and the alternative is guessing. Without them, every external channel is aggregated into a single undifferentiated bucket and you will never know which creator was worth paying.

The rest of the external playbook

Social accounts on the details page

Add YouTube, TikTok, X and Discord links to your game page. Post updates, teasers and screenshots regularly; reply to larger accounts in your space to grow reach.

A community, not an audience

A place for feedback, bug reports, suggestions and fan art. Roblox frames this as a retention engine — it keeps existing players engaged rather than finding new ones.

Creators and other developers

Partner with developers making similar games to grow together. Influencer reach is real but treat micro-creators as the efficient tier, not the consolation prize.

06Paid: what the numbers mean

Ads Manager covers sponsored experiences, search ads and immersive portal ads. The reporting is straightforward but the attribution rules are not, and they are where the confusion lives.

MetricDefinition
ImpressionsTimes your ad was shown
ClicksTimes players clicked your ad on Home or Search
PlaysTimes players entered and started playing as a result of the campaign
CPP (cost per play)Total spend divided by plays. The number to optimise
PlaytimeTotal time players spent in your game as a result of the campaign
Robux earningsCumulative Robux attributed to the campaign — includes ad revenue and Creator Rewards, excludes subscriptions

Attribution timing: impressions and clicks report in real time, but plays and earnings rely on attribution and can include events up to 30 days after an ad is served. A campaign that looks unprofitable on day two may not be. Judge campaigns on a 30-day window, not a daily one.

Resurrection credit: if a player returns via an ad after 7+ days away, they count as resurrected by that ad, and their plays and Robux spend are credited to it for the next 7 days. Campaign performance in the All Users view can therefore look weak while Recent Users or Resurrected Users shows the real effect. Check all three views before killing a campaign.

The thing paid traffic cannot do

None of it feeds the ranking stage. Ads are for revenue, for reaching a specific audience, and for getting into the retrieval candidate pool in the first place. If your plan is “buy players until the algorithm notices,” the algorithm has been explicitly built not to notice.
